# WBS-20 Evidence, Provenance and Audit Handover

WP-010 hands over requirement/decision/test/local-evidence references and custody requirements only.

WBS-20 retains exclusive downstream responsibility for institutional Evidence, canonical provenance/audit, custody, retention/legal hold and defensibility. WP-010 creates no institutional Evidence, Finding, canonical audit record, custody state or retention policy and grants WBS-20 no implementation authority.

Interim boundary: local evidence remains explicitly non-institutional and non-acceptance evidence.
